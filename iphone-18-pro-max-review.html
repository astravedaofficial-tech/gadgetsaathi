iPhone 18 Pro Max Review: Kya Yeh Upgrade Lene Layak Hai?

Updated Sept 20266 min read

iPhone 18 Pro Max, Apple ka sabse naya flagship, 9 September 2026 ko officially launch hua aur 18 September se India mein available hai. Is baar Apple ne battery aur camera dono pe bada focus kiya hai — chaliye dekhte hain kya yeh phone aapke paise ka sahi use hai.

Design aur Display

Phone mein 6.9-inch ka Super Retina XDR OLED display hai jo 120Hz refresh rate ke saath aata hai — scrolling aur gaming dono smooth feel hoti hai. Body Ceramic Shield glass se bani hai aur phone IP68 rated hai, matlab dust aur paani se surakshit (30 minute tak 6 meter gehrai mein).

Performance

Andar hai Apple ka naya A20 Pro chip, 2nm process pe bana — abhi tak ka sabse efficient Apple chip. 12GB RAM ke saath multitasking aur heavy apps bilkul lag-free chalti hain. Yeh chip Apple Intelligence (AI features) ko bhi better handle karta hai.

Camera

Triple 48MP camera setup hai — primary, ultra-wide aur telephoto (16x optical zoom tak). Low-light photography mein pichle models se noticeable improvement hai. Front camera bhi upgrade hoke 18MP ho gaya hai, selfies aur video calls sharp aati hain.

Battery Life

Yeh iss saal ka sabse bada upgrade hai — battery capacity 5,391mAh (India variant) tak badh gayi hai, jo iPhone 17 Pro Max se kaafi zyada hai. Heavy use mein bhi ek din aasani se nikal jata hai. USB-C port ke saath fast charging aur MagSafe wireless charging (25W tak) bhi milta hai.
