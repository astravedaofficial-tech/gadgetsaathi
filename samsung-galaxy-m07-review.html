Samsung Galaxy M07 Review: Best Budget Phone 2026 Mein?

Updated Sept 20265 min read

Samsung Galaxy M07, entry-level segment mein Samsung ka latest budget phone hai. Iska sabse bada highlight hai — 6 saal tak OS updates ka promise, jo is price range mein bilkul unheard-of hai. Chaliye dekhte hain kya yeh phone daily use ke liye sahi choice hai.

Design aur Display

Phone halka (184g) aur slim (7.6mm) hai, roz use karne mein comfortable lagta hai. 6.7-inch HD+ LCD display 90Hz refresh rate ke saath aata hai — is price range mein smooth scrolling ke liye achha hai. IP54 rating bhi hai, matlab halki dust aur splash se protection milta hai.

Performance

MediaTek Helio G99 processor, 4GB RAM ke saath — yeh chip casual use, social media aur halke games ke liye kaafi hai. Heavy gaming ya multitasking mein thodi limitation mehsoos ho sakti hai, lekin daily tasks (calls, WhatsApp, YouTube, camera) bilkul smooth chalte hain. 64GB storage microSD card se 2TB tak expand ho sakta hai — yeh badi baat hai is price mein.

Camera

50MP main camera + 2MP depth sensor ka setup hai, front mein 8MP selfie camera. Achi roshni mein photos decent aati hain; low-light mein expectations zyada na rakhein — yeh budget segment ka phone hai, flagship nahi.

Battery aur Software

5,000mAh battery 25W fast charging ke saath — normal use mein poora din aasani se nikal jata hai. Sabse impressive baat: Samsung ne 6 saal tak Android/security updates ka commitment diya hai (Android 15, One UI 7 ke saath) — yeh is price range ke kisi bhi competitor se zyada hai.
